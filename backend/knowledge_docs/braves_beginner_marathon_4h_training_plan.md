# Zalando 42.2 Challenge by Berlin Braves

This is a 12-week marathon plan for beginners and first-time marathoners targeting a finish time above four hours.

## Standard weekly structure

- Monday: optional strength training.
- Tuesday: track fast-interval session; most weeks total 6 km including warm-up and cool-down.
- Wednesday: 45-minute easy walk plus mandatory strength training.
- Thursday: threshold or mixed workout, commonly 10 km including warm-up and cool-down.
- Friday: rest.
- Saturday: easy run, generally 5–6 km.
- Sunday: long run at easy pace.

## Pace and effort guidance

- Marathon pace: the runner’s target marathon goal pace.
- Half-marathon pace: approximately 10 seconds per kilometre slower than 10K pace; effort around 6.5/10.
- 10K pace: effort around 7/10.
- 5K pace: effort around 8/10.
- 3K pace: effort around 8.5/10.
- 1K pace: effort around 9/10.
- Equal rest means the recovery duration equals the time taken to run one interval.
- Easy and long-run distances are shown inside the original session icons.
