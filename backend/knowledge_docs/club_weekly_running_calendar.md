# Berlin Braves Weekly Running Calendar

Club schedules can change. Members should confirm each session in the latest official club announcement before travelling.

## Weekly overview

| Day | Session | Meeting time | Start time | Approx. duration | Location | Audience |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Tuesday | Track Tuesday | 18:45 | 19:00 | 1 hour | ABC Zentrum Track | Runners doing coached tempo work |
| Thursday | Threshold Thursday | Usually 18:30 | Announcement-dependent | 1 hour | Bag drop near Mauerpark; exact address shared privately | Advanced runners |
| Saturday | Breakfast Miles | 08:45 | 09:00 sharp | 1 hour | Braves HQ, Wassertorstraße 11 | Community / social runners |

## Breakfast Miles

- Frequency: every Saturday.
- Format: community social run.
- Distance: usually 6–8 km.
- Meet: 08:45.
- Start: 09:00 sharp.
- Location: Braves HQ, Wassertorstraße 11.
- After the run: fresh coffee is prepared; runners may bring snacks to share.
- Character: social, community-focused, and suitable for conversational running.

## Track Tuesday

- Frequency: every Tuesday.
- Coach named in the supplied announcement: Coach Crish.
- Format: coached tempo session; the exact workout may change weekly.
- Meet: 18:45.
- Start: 19:00.
- Location: ABC Zentrum Track.
- Reminder: arrive hydrated, especially in warm weather.

## Threshold Thursday

- Frequency: described as a Thursday session.
- Intended audience: advanced runners.
- Typical meet time: 18:30.
- Approximate duration: 1 hour.
- Bag drop: a private address near Mauerpark.
- Exact location: request it through the club’s current private communication channel.
- Workout: varies by week; examples are stored in `tuesday_club_session_formats.md`.

## Calendar answering rules

- If asked for “this week’s workout,” say that the recurring calendar is known but the latest official club announcement must be checked.
- Never expose or invent Mari’s private address.
- Do not assume an example dated in April or May is the current workout.
- Do not state that every session is public; membership access can apply.
