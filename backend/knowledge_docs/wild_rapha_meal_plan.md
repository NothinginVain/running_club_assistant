# Wild Rapha Meal Plan for Runners

## Scope

This coach-authored guide presents general fueling principles and three example meal plans. It is not personalized medical or dietary advice. Energy needs vary with body size, training load, health, goals, and dietary preferences.

## Nutrition philosophy

- Favor minimally processed, single-ingredient foods for roughly 80% of intake, with flexibility for the remainder.
- Prioritize sufficient total energy and protein to support training and recovery.
- Increase carbohydrate intake as mileage or workout intensity rises; reduce it somewhat on lighter recovery days.
- Include dietary fats to support general health and recovery.
- The guide proposes a daily protein range of approximately 1.6-2.2 g/kg of body weight.

## Energy terminology

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| BMR | Basal metabolic rate: estimated energy used to maintain essential body functions at rest. |
| NEAT | Non-exercise activity thermogenesis: energy used through ordinary movement such as walking and standing. |
| TDEE | Total daily energy expenditure: BMR plus NEAT plus exercise and other components of daily expenditure. The source writes “TTDE,” but the standard abbreviation is TDEE. |

### Example calculation from the source

| Component | Example energy |
| --- | ---: |
| BMR | About 1,800 kcal/day |
| NEAT | About 500-800 kcal/day |
| 10 km run | About 750 kcal |
| Strength session | About 300 kcal |
| Example total | About 3,400 kcal/day |

These are the author’s personal examples, not universal calorie estimates.

## Example meal plans

### Plan 1 - approximately 2,200 kcal

| Meal | Foods |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast | 3 scrambled eggs, half an avocado, 2 slices sourdough, olive oil |
| Lunch | Large salad with grilled chicken breast, greens, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, feta, olive oil, and seeds |
| Snack | Greek yogurt, mixed berries, and a small handful of nuts |
| Dinner | Salmon fillet, quinoa, steamed broccoli, and olive oil |

Source estimate: about 160 g protein, 210 g carbohydrate, and 70 g fat.

### Plan 2 - approximately 2,700 kcal

| Meal | Foods |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast | 3 eggs, half an avocado, 2 slices sourdough, and a small serving of smoked salmon |
| Snack | Greek yogurt, granola, banana, and nuts |
| Lunch | Salad with grilled salmon, mixed greens, sweet potato, seeds, olive oil, and balsamic vinegar |
| Snack | Rice cake with nut butter and 1 boiled egg |
| Dinner | Lean beef or turkey mince, rice, and roasted root vegetables |

Source estimate: about 170 g protein, 260 g carbohydrate, and 80 g fat.

### Plan 3 - approximately 3,200 kcal

| Meal | Foods |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast | 4 eggs, half an avocado, 2 slices buttered sourdough, smoked salmon, and fruit |
| Snack | Greek yogurt, berries, granola, nuts, and dark chocolate |
| Lunch | Large salad with a double serving of chicken or salmon, quinoa, avocado, nuts, seeds, and olive oil |
| Snack | 2 rice cakes with nut butter plus trail mix or half a protein bar |
| Dinner | Large serving of salmon or lean beef, sweet potato or rice, and sauteed greens |

Source estimate: about 180 g protein, 320 g carbohydrate, and 100 g fat.
