## Routing boundaries

- Medical diagnosis, persistent pain, chest pain, fainting, or acute injury: route to a qualified healthcare professional, not a demo coach.
- Eating-disorder concerns, allergies, medical nutrition therapy, or supplements affected by medication: route to a qualified clinician.
- Official membership questions: use `hello@berlinbraves.com`, which is published on the club website.
- Current weekly logistics: verify the latest private club announcement.
