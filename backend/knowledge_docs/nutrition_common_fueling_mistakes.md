# Common Pre-Run and Race Fueling Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it can fail | Better approach |
|---|---|---|
| Eating high-fibre, high-fat food immediately before running | Slower digestion may contribute to heaviness or gastrointestinal distress | Move these foods earlier and use simpler carbohydrate near the run |
| Running on black coffee alone | Caffeine may increase alertness, but coffee supplies virtually no carbohydrate | Pair coffee with familiar carbohydrate when the session requires fuel |
| Trying new fuel on race day | Unknown taste, texture, dose, or tolerance creates avoidable risk | Rehearse products, meals, drinks, and timing during training |
| Treating hydration as a last-minute task | Starting dehydrated makes fluid replacement harder | Drink regularly across the day and adapt for heat, duration, and sweat losses |
| Waiting for an energy crash before fueling | Fuel takes time to consume and absorb | Begin the practised fueling schedule before major fatigue develops |

## Race-day rule

Nothing new on race day: use a plan that has already been tested during relevant training sessions.
