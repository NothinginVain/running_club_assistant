# Pre-Run Fueling by Time Before the Run

The available time determines the most practical food choice. As the run gets closer, prioritize easily digested carbohydrate and reduce fat, fibre, and portion size.

| Time before running | Main priority | Example options |
|---|---|---|
| 10-20 minutes | Very quick energy with minimal digestion | Juice, dates, gummy sweets, chews, or another small fast-carbohydrate option |
| 60-90 minutes | Light carbohydrate-focused meal or snack | Toast with jam, banana, low-fibre cereal, or another familiar breakfast |
| 90-120 minutes | More substantial carbohydrate top-up | Toast and banana, cereal, pretzels, or another tested carbohydrate snack |

## Selection rules

- Use foods already tested during training.
- Make the portion smaller when less time is available.
- Reduce fat and fibre before hard running if they cause heaviness or gastrointestinal distress.
- Coffee may support alertness, but it does not replace carbohydrate fuel.
- Avoid testing a new meal, gel, or drink on race day.

## Safety boundary

Repeated dizziness, faintness, or unusual weakness during exercise should not be treated only as a fueling problem. Stop or reduce exercise and seek medical assessment when symptoms are persistent, severe, or concerning.
