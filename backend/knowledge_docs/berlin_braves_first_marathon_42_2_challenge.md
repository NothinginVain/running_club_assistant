# Zalando 42.2 Challenge by Berlin Braves

## Plan overview

This is a 12-week plan for beginners and first-time marathoners targeting a finish above four hours. The standard rhythm combines optional Monday strength, Tuesday speed work, Wednesday easy walking plus mandatory strength, Thursday threshold or mixed work, Friday rest, a short Saturday run, and a Sunday long run.

Monthly challenge totals shown in the source: 153 km after week 4, 180 km after week 8, and 160 km after week 12.

## Plan progression

- Weeks 4 and 8 reduce the listed weekly volume and add extra rest.
- Week 10 has the highest listed training volume at 62 km and the longest training run at 35 km.
- Week 11 reduces the listed volume to 42 km before race week.
- Week 12 includes a 4 km shake-out followed by the 42.2 km marathon.

## Data-quality note

The source legend distinguishes optional strength, easy walk, easy run, track fast intervals, threshold sessions, mixed workouts, and long runs. Easy-run and long-run distances are displayed inside their session icons. The small text in the source image is difficult to read in a few interval cells. Where a workout could not be confirmed confidently, the transcription uses the clearly visible structure or labels it generically; ambiguous details should be checked against the supporting image before coaching use. Weekly totals are transcribed exactly as displayed, although some do not equal a simple sum of the visible running distances.

## Safety note

This is a high-volume plan with long runs up to 35 km and multiple quality sessions. It should not be assigned automatically without confirming recent weekly distance, long-run history, injury status, recovery capacity, and medical considerations.
