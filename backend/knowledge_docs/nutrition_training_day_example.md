# Example Fueled Training Day for an Evening Runner

This example shows how meals and snacks can support a run at 17:30. It is an illustration, not a fixed meal plan.

| Time | Meal or activity | Example |
|---:|---|---|
| 08:00 | Breakfast | Oatmeal with soy milk, banana, maple syrup, and walnuts |
| 12:30 | Lunch | Rice bowl with baked tofu, mixed vegetables, and tahini dressing |
| 15:30 | Pre-run fuel | Toast with banana |
| 17:30 | Training | Running session |
| 18:45 | Immediate recovery | Chocolate soy milk or fruit smoothie with protein |
| 20:00 | Dinner | Roasted sweet potato, protein source, cooked greens, and olive oil |

## Why the schedule works

- Breakfast and lunch provide baseline daily energy.
- The pre-run snack reduces the long gap between lunch and training.
- Immediate recovery combines carbohydrate and protein.
- Dinner completes recovery with a substantial mixed meal.

## Vegetarian protein substitutions

Tofu, tempeh, seitan, legumes, eggs, dairy products, or soy foods can replace a meat-based protein source according to dietary preference and tolerance.
