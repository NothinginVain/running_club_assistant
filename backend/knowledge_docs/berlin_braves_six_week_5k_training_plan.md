# Berlin Braves Six-Week 5K Training Plan

## Plan structure

- Monday: 30-minute easy run plus six 20-second strides, RPE 4–5.
- Tuesday: fast interval session, RPE 9.
- Wednesday: off.
- Thursday: threshold interval session, RPE 7–8.
- Friday: heavy strength training.
- Saturday: Breakfast Miles, 10 km.
- Sunday: optional cross-training, RPE 1–4.

## Weekly sessions

### Week 1 — June 15–21

- Tuesday: warm-up; 10 × 1 minute fast with 2 minutes slow jogging or walking; cool-down.
- Thursday: warm-up; 4 × 1,000 m with 90 seconds of easy jogging between repetitions; cool-down.

### Week 2 — June 22–28

- Tuesday: warm-up; 10 × 1 minute fast with 2 minutes slow jogging or walking; cool-down.
- Thursday: warm-up; 4 × 1,400 m with 90 seconds of easy jogging between repetitions; cool-down.

### Week 3 — June 29–July 5

- Tuesday: warm-up; 8 × 250 m fast with 90 seconds recovery; cool-down.
- Thursday: warm-up; 4 × 1,400 m with 60 seconds of easy jogging between repetitions; cool-down.

### Week 4 — July 6–12

- Tuesday: warm-up; 8 × 250 m fast with 90 seconds recovery; cool-down.
- Thursday: warm-up; 4 × 1,800 m with 120 seconds of easy jogging between repetitions; cool-down.

### Week 5 — July 13–19

- Tuesday: warm-up; 5 × 100 m fast with 2 minutes recovery, then 4 × 500 m fast with 2 minutes recovery; cool-down.
- Thursday: warm-up; 4 × 1,800 m with 90 seconds of easy jogging between repetitions; cool-down.

### Week 6 — July 20–25

- Tuesday: warm-up; 5 × 100 m fast with 2 minutes recovery, then 4 × 500 m fast with 2 minutes recovery; cool-down.
- Thursday: warm-up; 3 × 1,000 m with 90 seconds of easy jogging between repetitions; cool-down.

## Intensity guide

- RPE 1–4: very easy to easy effort, suitable for optional cross-training and recovery.
- RPE 4–5: comfortable easy-running effort.
- RPE 7–8: hard, controlled threshold effort.
- RPE 9: very hard interval effort.
