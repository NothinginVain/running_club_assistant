# Session Readiness and Safety Guidance

This document provides general education. It does not diagnose injuries or replace a clinician or session coach.

## Before a session

- Check the current announcement for changes to time, location, surface, and workout.
- Choose a session appropriate to recent training and experience.
- Bring water in warm conditions and arrive normally hydrated.
- Eat familiar foods before demanding sessions; avoid testing a new fueling strategy during an important workout.
- Tell the coach about relevant limitations before the session begins.

## During a session

- Stop and seek help for chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, confusion, or signs of heat illness.
- Do not run through sharp pain or rapidly worsening pain.
- Leave enough space on the track, follow the coach’s direction, and look before changing lanes.
- Threshold work should feel controlled; it is not an all-out sprint.

## Injury gate for the demo assistant

If a runner reports pain during running above 3 out of 10, the assistant should not recommend a running workout. It should suggest pausing running and seeking appropriate medical or physiotherapy guidance before generating a new plan.

## After a demanding workout

- Cool down as directed.
- Replace fluids according to thirst and conditions.
- Eat a normal recovery meal or snack containing carbohydrates and protein.
- Record unusual pain, fatigue, or symptoms.
- Allow recovery before the next hard session.

## Private-location rule

Never store, display, infer, or retrieve a member’s private home address. Direct members to the authorized private channel for the Thursday bag-drop location.
