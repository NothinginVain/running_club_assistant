# Braves Session Formats and Historical Workout Examples

These examples came from dated club announcements. They explain possible formats but must not be presented as the current week’s program.

## Tempo running

Tempo work is sustained faster running performed with control. Track Tuesday is described as a coached tempo session, but its exact workout is announced separately.

## Example: Threshold 400s

- Warm-up and drills: not specified in the supplied message.
- Main set: 14–16 × 400 m at upper-threshold effort.
- Recovery: 60 seconds of easy jogging after each repetition.
- Audience: advanced runners familiar with threshold training.

## Example: Fast 300s

- Intended for runners currently preparing for middle-distance races.
- Main set: 4 sets of 3 × 300 m.
- Between repetitions: 100 m shuffle.
- Between sets: 3 minutes.

## Example: Threshold 800s

- Historical date in source: 9 April.
- Meet: 18:30.
- Start: 18:45.
- Structure:
  1. 15-minute warm-up.
  2. Running drills and strides.
  3. 8–10 × 800 m at threshold effort.
  4. 100 m walk or jog between repetitions.
  5. 10-minute cool-down.

## What to bring

- Water or an appropriate hydration drink.
- Fuel if needed for the session and the runner’s individual requirements.
- A recovery drink or snack for after demanding sessions.
- Weather-appropriate clothing.
- Track-appropriate running shoes.

## Important interpretation

“Upper threshold” and “threshold effort” are intensity descriptions, not a universal pace. A coach should individualize the pace. Runners should not copy an advanced workout without considering their experience, current training load, health, and recovery.
