# Underfueling and REDs Warning Signs in Runners

**REDs** means **Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport**. It can develop when energy intake is insufficient for both training and essential body functions. It can affect athletes of any gender.

## Warning signs highlighted in the workshop

- persistent or chronic fatigue;
- poor sleep;
- missed or disrupted menstrual periods;
- recurrent stress fractures or bone-stress injuries;
- repeated joint or injury problems;
- mood changes or anxiety;
- unexplained or sudden performance decline.

## Why underfueling matters

Restricting carbohydrate or total energy during heavy training can negatively affect:

- recovery and training adaptation;
- hormone function;
- bone health;
- muscle retention;
- mood and general wellbeing;
- athletic performance.

Becoming lighter does not automatically make a runner faster. Intentional restriction can worsen health and performance.

## What to do

Do not self-diagnose REDs. When warning signs are persistent or concerning:

1. reduce training stress when appropriate;
2. seek assessment from a clinician or sports dietitian familiar with REDs;
3. obtain urgent medical care for fainting, chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, or other acute symptoms.
